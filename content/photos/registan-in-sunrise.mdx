I arrived at Registan before sunrise. The square was almost empty and the city was still asleep.

The weather forecast predicted clear skies, but the horizon was covered with thin clouds. For a moment I thought the sunrise would be disappointing.

![Waiting for Light](/photos/registan/01.jpg)

After nearly forty minutes of waiting, the first light finally appeared. It illuminated only the top section of the madrasah while the rest remained in shadow.

That contrast is what made this photograph special.

![Morning Light](/photos/registan/02.jpg)

As more people started arriving, the quiet atmosphere disappeared. Looking back, waking up at 5 a.m. was completely worth it.
