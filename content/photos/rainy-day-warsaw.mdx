![Warsaw Rain](/photos/warsaw-rain/cover.jpg)

I wasn't planning to take any photos that day.

The weather had been terrible since the morning and I almost stayed home.

![Street Reflection](/photos/warsaw-rain/01.jpg)

While walking through the Old Town, I noticed how the wet pavement reflected the warm lights from nearby restaurants.

The rain transformed an ordinary street into something cinematic.

![Old Town](/photos/warsaw-rain/02.jpg)

Some of my favorite photographs happen when I stop looking for perfect conditions and simply react to what's in front of me.
